# India CPI Inflation Analysis Using Excel

## Project Overview
This project analyses India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) data to understand inflation trends, category-wise contributions and the impact of major economic events.

## Business Objectives
- Identify broader categories contributing to CPI.
- Analyse year-over-year inflation trends.
- Investigate food inflation patterns.
- Understand changes in inflation before and after COVID-19.
- Examine the relationship between imported crude oil price fluctuations and CPI categories.

## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- SUMIFS
- INDEX-MATCH
- AVERAGE
- CORREL
- Percentage Change Analysis

## Analysis Performed
1. Category-wise contribution towards CPI.
2. Year-over-year inflation analysis.
3. Monthly food inflation analysis.
4. Pre- and post-COVID inflation comparison.
5. Crude oil price correlation analysis.

## Key Findings
- Food was the highest CPI contributor, accounting for approximately 46% in the analysed dataset.
- 2019 recorded the highest annual inflation at approximately 7.7%.
- Analysed monthly food inflation movements and identified significant changes.
- Examined inflation patterns surrounding the COVID-19 period.
- Investigated correlations between imported crude oil prices and CPI categories.
